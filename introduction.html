We are animal keepers in a zoo and responsible for feeding the monkeys.

The monkeys look very cute, but we have to be careful because some of the monkeys are biting.

So what we have here is a set of monkeys for which we know whether they bite or they do not bite.

As we can see, the monkeys differ in certain characteristics. We call those features, such as whether they smile, if they blink, or the shape of their eyes.
